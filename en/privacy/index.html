Privacy policy

Barusik Platforms · barusik.com

This is a translation. If there is any difference, the Hebrew version applies.

This policy explains what information Barusik Platforms (barusik.com, Bar Lvovski Travel, Platformika, Complichess, Barusik Crypto, LearnLume and What Penina thinks) collects, why, and what your rights are. The services are run by Bar Lvovski (Barusik Platforms).

What we collect

Shared account: email, username and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash — nobody, including us, can read it.

Platformika: your profile (name, bio, pictures), posts, clips, stories, comments, likes, private messages, blocks and reports.

Complichess: player name, rating and game data.

Barusik Crypto: your access request, your deposit addresses, deposits (transaction id and amount), balances, and withdrawal requests including the destination address and transaction id. The transactions themselves are public on the Bitcoin blockchain; on Monero they are private.

What Penina thinks: the chat you upload opens on your device, and the stats are computed there. For the full report a large excerpt of the chat (up to the whole chat) is sent, with phone numbers and emails masked and contacts that appear only as a phone number replaced with a label. This excerpt is not stored — not by us and not in logs. Before writing, Penina may ask you a few questions; your answers are kept with the report. We keep the request (when you asked, how many messages and people the chat has, whether it was approved), the finished report, including the mini-games built from it, and any photos you add to the report. A report may contain names and short quotes from the chat.

LearnLume: runs on your computer. Your files go straight from your computer to Claude with your own key and never pass through us.

Bar Lvovski Travel: when you send a request or booking — name, phone, email (optional), notes and trip details. Searches are kept briefly so identical searches can be answered quickly.

Payments: what was ordered, the amount, the coupon if any, the order and confirmation numbers, and your name, phone and email to arrange payment and send the receipt. For a bank transfer we only record that the order was paid; your bank details are not stored on the site. Once a card provider is connected, card details are entered with it and never reach us.

Technical data: to prevent flooding and abuse we briefly keep a one-way hash of your IP address — not the address itself. Hosting providers keep standard access logs.

How it is used

To run the services: sign-in, showing your content, messaging, games and ratings.

To handle travel requests and bookings and contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email.

To keep the services secure, stop spam and handle reports.

We do not sell data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising cookies.

What others can see

On Platformika your profile, posts, clips, comments and likes are public. Stories are visible for 24 hours. Private messages are visible only to the sender and recipient.

On Complichess your player name and rating may appear in tables. If you join a clan, its members see your name, trophies and when you were last active, and your clan chat messages are visible to them.

Travel requests are visible only to Bar.

What Penina thinks: your report is visible to you and the site operator. If you share the report’s private link, anyone who has the link can see the report, including its photos. You can switch the link off at any time, and it stops working.

Who we share it with

Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage. Servers may be outside Israel.

Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google": Google gives us the email, name and picture of your Google account. We never see your Google password.

GitHub Pages and Cloudflare — website hosting and domain.

Google Fonts — the site’s fonts load from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, so your IP address reaches Google.

Anthropic (Claude) — writes Penina’s reports from the excerpt that is sent (masked as described above). Anthropic does not train models on content sent through its API.

CoinGecko — the world prices shown on the exchange load straight from your browser, with no personal details.

BeAgent — travel searches, bookings and payment clearing for Bar Lvovski Travel run through BeAgent, which has its own terms and clearing. It receives the search and booking details this requires.

Travel suppliers (airlines, hotels, car rental, etc.) — only the booking details they need, and only when you ask to book.

The payment company — Allpay, Grow or Hyp (whichever is active at the time) — receives your name, phone and email to take the payment and issue a receipt.

Authorities — only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account data and content — until you delete your account.

Travel requests and bookings — as long as needed to handle them and as the law requires for records.

IP hashes for flood protection — up to one day. Temporary travel searches — up to two days.

Your rights

You can see, correct and ask us to delete your information.

Delete your account: in Platformika, open your profile → "Delete account". This deletes the shared account and all your content.

You can also write to us and we will handle it within 30 days.

Local browser storage

The sites keep your sign-in and preferences (language, accessibility settings) in your browser. There are no third-party cookies.

Children

Platformika is for ages 13 and up; under 18 with a parent’s consent. If we learn we hold data of a child under 13, we delete it.

Security

Access to every table is restricted on the server: each user sees and changes only what they are allowed to, and only the admin sees travel requests and reports. The admin page shows how many messages each user sent — never their content. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a problem, please tell us.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date on this page. Material changes will also be shown on the site.
